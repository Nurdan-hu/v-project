You can reach your contacts, save and delete contacts and edit your contacts.
<br>
My aim in this project  is to create a phone book application.
Pressing 1 on the keyboard you can see your persons.
Pressing 2 on the keyboard you can add person to your list.
Pressing 3 on the keyboard you can delete person.
Pressing 4 on the keyboard you can edit the phone numbers or persons name, surname.
